# Altiora AI: 7-day social plan (carousels)

Drafted 23 Nov 2026. Status: **waiting for the owner's approval. No credits spent.** Security first-pass fixes applied 23 Nov (alto/security/2026-11-23-ads-7day-first-pass.md on claude/security-reviews).

## Format for every post

- **Carousel**, 1080 × 1350 (4:5). Works on Instagram and LinkedIn (LinkedIn as a PDF document post).
- **Images:** generated with no text; every word is added afterwards with the brand fonts (Archivo headlines, Source Sans 3 body, Geist Mono for small labels).
- **Colours:** forest #0B1A13 / #0F2219, gold #D4AF37 / #E3B65E, off-white #EEF0E8. Recall slides use navy, soft blue #86B6D6 and gold #F0B85A. Tessera slides use gold #E3B65E and green #3C9A6C.
- **Look:** calm and premium, warm evening or morning light, generic Gulf architecture. Fictional people only. No real hotels, brands, landmarks or logos. No neon.
- **Every caption ends with:** "Some images are AI-generated and the people shown are fictional. Altiora AI is preparing to launch in the UAE."
- **Suggested times (Dubai):** LinkedIn 09:00, Instagram 19:30.
- **Every [GEN] slide** carries a small "AI-generated image" corner label (Geist Mono). Switch on Meta's "AI info" label on Instagram.
- **Organic posts only.** No paid boosts or ads until the trade licence is issued and any Media Council permit or exemption is confirmed.
- **Never add** "book", "quote" (as our offer), "sign up" or "DM for pricing". Reject any generated face that looks like a real or famous person.

Key: **[GEN]** = new Higgsfield image. **[BRAND]** = typeset brand slide. **[SCREEN]** = real product screen.
