Tessera

The room is ready before you reach the door.

Tessera turns check-in into the moment the room comes to life. The guest checks in from their phone; the hotel approves; the room powers on, cools to their chosen temperature and welcomes them by name. Their phone becomes the key to the lift, the room and the hotel, and the whole stay runs from there.

See how a stay works

The difference

Not a card on a phone. A stay that runs itself.

Replacing the key card with a phone is the easy part. Tessera connects check-in, the room, the key and the team, so every step the guest takes is already prepared for them.

Check in from the phone on arrival day. A person at the desk approves it.
A plastic card, cut and handed across the desk.
A key in the phone's wallet, shared with the rest of the party if the guest chooses.
Walk into a hot, dark room and feel for the card slot.
The room is already on, cooled and lit, with a welcome on the screen.
Card in the slot keeps the power on, even when nobody is in.
The room knows when the guest is in, eases into eco mode when they leave, and is ready again when they return.
Call reception for towels, a taxi or a late check-out.
Ask on WhatsApp in Arabic or English; the right team picks it up.
Queue again to check out and wait for the bill.
