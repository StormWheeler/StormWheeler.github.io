Check in from the phone, approved by a person

03 · The room wakes up

The moment check-in is approved, the room comes to life

Power on, air conditioning cooling to the guest's chosen temperature, lights set to a welcome scene, curtains open and their name on the screen. On a summer afternoon in the UAE, they walk into a room that is already cool.

  • Preferences from earlier stays are remembered
  • Every setting can be adjusted from the phone

04 · Straight to the room

One key for the lift, the room and the hotel

The key is in the phone's wallet, ready the moment check-in is approved. The guest skips the desk, taps the lift to their floor, taps the door, and uses the same key for the gym, pool or spa they have access to. They can share it with the people staying with them.

  • Works with the hotel's existing door locks
  • Cancelled instantly if a phone is lost

05 · A room that knows you are there

No card slot. Power when it matters.

Instead of a card in a slot, the room senses when the guest is in. When they leave, it eases into eco mode; when they come back, it is cool and lit again by the time they open the door. Overnight, it switches to a quiet night setting.

  • Comfort for the guest, lower energy use for the hotel
  • No cameras and no tracking: presence in the room only

06 · During the stay

Everything else, by message

Towels, a taxi, a late check-out: the guest asks on WhatsApp, in Arabic or English. Simple requests are handled straight away and go to the right team; anything involving money or exceptions goes to a person to approve. Housekeeping sees when the room is empty, so cleaning never disturbs the guest.
