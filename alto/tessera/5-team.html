For the hotel team

One view of every arrival, room and request

The front desk sees who has checked in, which rooms are ready and what needs a person. Staff can approve, override or issue a physical card at any moment. Housekeeping works from live room status instead of knocking on doors.

  • Approvals for anything involving ID, money or exceptions
  • Live room status: ready, occupied, away, eco, to clean
  • Runs in the hotel's own accounts; the data stays with the hotel

Built in, not bolted on

Designed around how hotels already run.

Works with what is there

Designed to connect to the hotel's booking system, door locks and room controls. Physical cards keep working as a backup.

A person approves what matters

ID checks, payments and anything unusual go to the hotel team. The system prepares; people decide.

Private by design

Keys are time-limited and cancelled at check-out. No location tracking and no cameras. Guest data stays in the hotel's accounts.

Arabic and English

Every message, screen and request works in both languages, from the first invitation to the final bill.

Where Tessera is today

In development, and looking for hotels to shape it.

We are working with a small number of UAE hotels and holiday-home operators to design Tessera around real operations before it goes anywhere near a guest. If what you have seen fits your property, we would value a conversation.
