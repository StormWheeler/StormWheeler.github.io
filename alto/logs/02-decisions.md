# 02 Decisions register: altioraai.com

Every decision that shapes the website: what was decided, who decided it, and why. Newest decisions are at the bottom. "Owner" means Storm Wheeler.

| # | Date | Decision | Decided by | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 15 Nov | Position Altiora AI as AI systems for UAE hospitality, events and venues, with a calm, premium brand | Owner | Focus on businesses where the guest experience is the product |
| 2 | 15 Nov | Write the site and legal pages as a planned business that is not yet licensed or trading; no services offered or sold, no prices | Owner, following the Legal Desk | Stay accurate and lawful before licensing |
| 3 | 15 Nov | Stay out of regulated fields (health, finance, security) | Owner | Keep the scope safe and simple |
| 4 | 15 Nov | Replace the Voiceflow assistant with a self-hosted Alto ("rebuild based on what the company plans to achieve") | Owner | Voiceflow returned no answers. Self-hosted means it always works, costs nothing to run, and nothing visitors type is stored |
| 5 | 15 Nov | Alto answers from scripted, pre-written content in English and Arabic, and hands over to a person on request | Claude, approved by owner | Predictable, accurate answers with no risk of an AI making things up about the business |
| 6 | 15 Nov | Use a free, built-in language switch instead of Webflow's paid Localization | Owner | No extra cost; works on every page |
| 7 | 15 Nov | Six languages: English, Arabic, Russian, Chinese (Simplified), Hindi, French. "Six is enough" | Owner | These cover the main visitor groups in the UAE |
| 8 | 15 Nov | Legal pages in other languages show a note that the English version prevails | Claude | Translations are for convenience; the English text is the legal one |
| 9 | 15 Nov | Form enquiries go to a Google Sheet in the business account (hello@altioraai.com), not the personal account | Owner | Keep the clientele list with the business |
| 10 | 15 Nov | Alerts go to Telegram, summary only (not WhatsApp, not the full message) | Owner | Instant alert without putting full personal details in chat |
| 11 | 15 Nov | Delete enquiries older than 24 months unless marked "Client" | Claude, matching the Privacy notice | Keep only what the Privacy notice says is kept |
| 12 | 16 Nov | Add "Altiora Labs" to the menu, with a Labs page listing projects | Owner | Show the products Altiora builds and owns |
| 13 | 16 Nov | Tessera gets its own page with a full animated walkthrough of how it works, without the nitty-gritty | Owner | Use the website as the place to present Tessera to hotels |
| 14 | 16 Nov | The Tessera page is visible on the site but password-protected | Owner | Protect the idea from being copied |
| 15 | 16 Nov | Tessera: the room wakes at check-in approval; features shown are presence power, WhatsApp concierge, express check-out and hotel team view | Owner | "Not just replacing a card with a phone, next level" |
| 16 | 16 Nov | Tessera page in English only, with a Present mode for meetings | Owner | Used in face-to-face meetings with hotels |
| 17 | 16 Nov | Online check-in opens on the day of arrival, and a person at the desk approves every ID | Claude, reflecting UAE guest registration rules | Lawful and realistic for UAE hotels |
| 18 | 16 Nov | Keep Tessera as an unpublished draft until the owner sets the password in the Webflow Designer | Claude | Webflow only lets the owner set page passwords; publishing first would have exposed the page |
| 19 | 16 Nov | Tessera content is kept only inside the protected page, never in public files or translation files | Claude | Nothing about Tessera's details can leak through public files |
| 20 | 16 Nov | Describe Tessera publicly as "a new guest experience for hotels", not "a mobile room key" | Claude, in line with owner's brief | The owner said it is more than replacing a card with a phone |
| 21 | 17 Nov | Alto and the website should know about Recall | Owner | Visitors asking about Recall were getting no answer |
| 22 | 17 Nov | Show the Recall beta as "Free beta soon", with joining by email for now, not the app link | Claude | The beta checklist showed the beta was not yet open |
| 23 | 17 Nov | Plan for the beta button: "Join the free beta" while places remain, switching automatically to "Request to join the beta" (an email with the subject filled in) once full | Owner | Let anyone who finds it join until the cap, then collect requests; the owner decides later when to move to a paid app |
| 24 | 17 Nov | The beta limit stays in Recall's own system (currently 105); the website only reads open/full and places left, never personal data | Claude | One source of truth; privacy |
| 25 | 17 Nov | If the website cannot check the beta status, show "Request to join" | Claude | Nobody is ever sent to a sign-up that turns them away |
| 26 | 17 Nov | The Recall app session gets a written brief to paste, because direct messaging between sessions is blocked | Owner ("if you can't communicate directly") | Allowing direct messaging would permanently restrict this session to trusted devices, so it was not switched on without asking |
| 27 | 17 Nov | Altiora Labs gets its own animation (the lab flask); each product keeps its own | Owner | Each part of Labs should feel distinct |
| 28 | 17 Nov | Each product can carry its own look inside its card, without disrupting the rest of the site | Owner | Products have their own identity; the site stays consistent |
| 29 | 17 Nov | Recall's card uses Recall's real icon and colours (navy, blue, gold); Tessera stays in the Altiora palette until it has its own logo | Owner and Claude | Recall already has a brand; Tessera's is not decided yet |
| 30 | 17 Nov | Keep a full log of changes and decisions on Google Drive under 06 Platform & Stack / Website – altioraai.com | Owner | A permanent, organised record |
| 31 | 17 Nov | Tessera gets its own icon; Altiora AI stays the parent brand ("Tessera, by Altiora AI") | Owner | Each product has its own identity, as Recall does |
| 32 | 17 Nov | Tessera's icon is the hospitality token, version 1B (single rounded tab, even seam) | Owner | Tells the story behind the name; the first, toothed version looked too aggressive and not professional enough |
| 33 | 17 Nov | Tessera's icon uses the Altiora forest colours only: deep green #0F2219, gold #E3B65E, green #3C9A6C. No other colour versions | Owner | Keeps Tessera close to the parent brand |
| 34 | 17 Nov | Every change to the website ends with a full site check (all pages, phone, tablet, desktop and Arabic) before it is reported as done | Owner | Fixing one thing had been breaking another |
