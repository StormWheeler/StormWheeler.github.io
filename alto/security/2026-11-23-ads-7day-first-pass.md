# Legal check: 7-day social carousel plan, first pass, 23 Nov 2026

**Reviewed:** `alto/ads/7-day-plan.md` (branch `claude/altiora-ads-7day`), all slide words, captions, hashtags and alt text.
**Verdict: FIX NEEDED (wording only).** Good base: no prices, offers, client names, testimonials or statistics; fictional people; no real hotels or landmarks.

## Findings

### Medium
1. **Present-tense "trading" claims.** Day 1 slide 2 and caption: "Altiora AI designs and runs…". Day 2 caption: "Altiora AI's guest systems answer…". Day 3 caption: "Altiora AI's team systems move…". Day 4 caption: "we map… build… test… keep looking after it". Day 5 alt text: "Altiora AI works with". Fixes:
   - D1 slide 2: "Altiora AI is preparing to design and run AI systems for UAE hotels, holiday homes, venues and event teams."
   - D1 caption: "Altiora AI is preparing to design and run the quiet systems behind them…"
   - D2 caption: "Altiora AI's guest systems are designed to answer enquiries…"
   - D3 caption: "Altiora AI's team systems are designed to move those details…"
   - D4 caption: "How we'll work: we'll map your operation first, build one system per job, test it before any guest sees it, and keep looking after it." Slide 6 can stay.
   - D5 alt text: "…each naming a type of business Altiora AI plans to work with."
   - Capability slides (D2, D3, D5): add a small label "What we're designing" on slide 1 or 2 of each.
2. **AI disclosure (Q1).** The caption line is good but not enough on its own. Captions are truncated, LinkedIn document posts show slides without the caption, and the line is wrong for the real Recall screen (Day 6 slide 3).
   - Caption: "Some images are AI-generated and the people shown are fictional. Altiora AI is preparing to launch in the UAE."
   - Each GEN slide: a small corner label "AI-generated image".
   - Instagram: switch on Meta's "AI info" label for the photorealistic images.
3. **Day 7 (Q3): "We're talking to hotels, venues and event teams now."** This reads as live sales activity or existing relationships, before a licence. It's only defensible if it's true and no deals are being discussed. Safer: slide 3 "Planned, not yet trading. We'd love to hear from hotels, venues and event teams." Caption: "…we're planned, not yet trading, and we'd like to hear from hotels, holiday homes, venues and event teams about where AI could quietly take work off their plate…"
4. **Recall "Live now" (Q4).** The live Labs page still says "Free beta soon", and the Day 6 CTA sends people to altioraai.com to join. Post this only once the site says the same thing and the join button works. Otherwise put the direct app link in the caption or bio. Keep "A training app, not a medical product" on slide 3 and in the caption; both already have it, which is good.
5. **UAE media rules (owner to confirm).** The UAE Media Law (Federal Decree-Law 55 of 2023) and the Media Council's advertising standards apply to social posts. The Council also has an advertiser permit regime for promotional content on social media. A business posting about itself on its own accounts is generally treated differently from paid influencer content, but Altiora isn't licensed yet. Recommendation:
   - organic posts only, with no paid boosts or ads, until the trade licence is issued
   - the owner confirms with the Media Council (or the Legal Desk) whether any permit or exemption applies before paid promotion

### Low
6. **#WhatsApp (Q8).** Drop the hashtag. A trademark hashtag on brand content can suggest a partnership, and Meta's brand rules discourage it. Plain-text "WhatsApp" describing a channel is fine. Never show the WhatsApp logo.
7. **CTAs (Q2).** "Follow along", "Coming to the UAE" and "Join the free beta" (Recall only) are fine. "Talk to us at altioraai.com" is acceptable because the site states the pre-licence position on every page. Don't add "book", "get a quote", "sign up" or "DM for pricing".
8. **Hooks and capability lines (Q6, Q7).** Fine as descriptions of what is designed, with fix 1. "Quotes" on Day 5 is fine (the venue's own quotes). "Built for the UAE's guest-facing teams" → "Designed for the UAE's guest-facing teams".
9. **Tessera (Q5).** Fine: mood only, no features, partners or prices. Don't add "with a small number of properties".
10. **Brand name.** Before spending on reach, run a UAE trademark search (Ministry of Economy) for "Altiora" in classes 9 and 42, and consider filing.
11. **Fictional people.** Generated faces must not resemble real or famous people. Reject any draft that does.
