# Security & compliance review: baseline, 23 Nov 2026

**Scope:** live altioraai.com (all 10 public pages, /tessera, /401, 404, robots, sitemap, response headers), site-wide code in `alto/` (head, footer, i18n, Alto), the approved redesign `alto/redo/home-redesign-preview.html`, and the public repository.
**Reviewer:** Security & compliance session. Advisory only; no systems were changed.

## Verdict: FIX NEEDED (one High item, outside the website itself)

The live site is in good shape: no trackers or analytics, no secret keys in public code, HTTPS enforced with HSTS, forms have consent ticks, Alto keeps everything in the browser and renders typed text safely, the redesign stores only a validated choice in localStorage and respects reduced motion. Pre-licence footers and legal pages are consistent.

## Findings

### High

**H1. Confidential product material is in the public repository.**
- *Where:* this repository is public (`visibility: public`). Working files for the password-protected page, and logs describing it, are readable by anyone without a password.
- *Why:* the page password protects nothing while the same content is public here. Decision 19 ("never in public files") is not being met. Also visible: internal test-database identifiers, a demo sign-in address, app bug findings, Drive folder links and video job IDs.
- *Fix:* the owner decides one of: (a) make the repository private (GitHub Pages for this repo is not used by altioraai.com, which runs on Webflow), or (b) move the confidential working files and handovers to a private repository and remove them from this one, including from history. Note that anything already public may have been copied; treat the current material as seen. Until then, add nothing further about the private product here. (Details given to the owner directly, not in this file.)

### Medium

**M1. Present-tense "trading" wording.** Home (live and redesign): "Altiora AI designs and runs AI systems for…". About: "every client interaction". Labs: "Alongside the systems we set up for clients". These contradict "not yet licensed or trading" and imply existing clients.
*Fix:* "Altiora AI is preparing to design and run AI systems for…"; About: "every interaction"; Labs: "Alongside the systems we plan to build for businesses".

**M2. Redesign: claims of current customers or results.** "in private preview with a small number of properties" (Tessera teaser) and the "What a quiet system does" timeline ("Answered in Arabic, in seconds"). Pre-licence, this reads as live deployments with real properties.
*Fix:* Tessera: "A new guest experience for hotels, in private preview." Timeline: add a caption "Illustrative examples of what our systems are designed to do. Not client results."

**M3. Leftover Calendly script on the home page.** The home page body still loads `assets.calendly.com/widget.js` and CSS and defines a global `openCalendly(url)` for the removed Voiceflow bot. It sends every home visitor's IP and browser details to Calendly, and Calendly is not in the Privacy notice.
*Fix:* delete that embed (Home page settings → custom code, before `</body>`). Then remove assets.calendly.com from the QA allowed-host notes.

**M4. Cookie notice and Privacy notice don't match what the site does.**
- The redesign stores "What brings you here?" (`altiora-path`) in localStorage; the Cookie notice mentions only the language choice.
- Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com, fonts.gstatic.com, ajax.googleapis.com webfont loader) and Cloudflare (sets a `_cfuvid` cookie, via Webflow) receive visitor IPs but are not named.
- Form submissions currently stay in Webflow (pipeline not connected), but the notice says they go to Google Sheets and Telegram. Over-disclosure is fine, but keep it accurate when the pipeline connects.
*Fix (before the redesign goes live):* Cookie notice, under "Chat assistant and language": "…If you choose an answer to 'What brings you here?', your browser keeps that choice on your device (local storage, key `altiora-path`) so the page stays arranged for you. It is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes it." Add under Essential: "Our host's security provider sets a short-lived cookie (`_cfuvid`) to protect the site." Privacy notice §3 add: "Google Fonts: delivers the site's typefaces; Google receives your IP address and browser details when a page loads." Better still, self-host the fonts in Webflow (upload Archivo, Source Sans 3 and Geist Mono as custom fonts) and drop the Google Fonts lines.

**M5. Recall messaging is inconsistent across the site.** Labs says "Free beta soon" and "100 free places"; the redesign says "Live now" / "out now"; Alto describes old exercises (Sequence, N-back, Hold) and "getting ready for a free beta, with 100 places"; the beta limit is 105. A visitor sees three different stories.
*Fix:* pick one status and use it on Labs, Alto (EN and AR) and the redesign at the same time. Update Alto's exercise list to Settle, Lane, Watch, Words. Don't state a place count, or use the live figure.

**M6. Enquiry pipeline (not yet connected): formula injection.** `alto/enquiries/Code.gs` writes form text straight into Google Sheets with `appendRow`. A value starting with `=`, `+`, `-` or `@` is run as a formula (for example an `IMPORTDATA` that leaks sheet contents when opened).
*Fix:* before `appendRow`, wrap each user field: `function safe(v){v=String(v||'');return /^[=+\-@\t\r]/.test(v)?"'"+v:v;}` and use `safe(row.name)`, `safe(row.email)`, `safe(row.phone)`, `safe(row.message)`, `safe(p.publishedPath)`.

### Low

- **L1. No clickjacking protection.** No `X-Frame-Options` or CSP `frame-ancestors` header. Webflow can't set headers on this plan. *Fix:* add to head code: `<script>if(top!==self){try{top.location=self.location}catch(e){document.documentElement.style.display='none'}}</script>` (the Recall app already has a frame guard).
- **L2. Form spam.** Contact and Connect use Webflow forms with no honeypot. *Fix:* turn on Webflow's built-in spam filtering (Site settings → Forms) rather than reCAPTCHA, which would add Google. Before connecting the pipeline, also cap field lengths server-side in `handleSubmission` (e.g. 5,000 characters for the message).
- **L3. Webhook key in the URL.** The Apps Script key travels as `?key=` and will appear in Webflow's webhook settings and Google logs. That's acceptable for this use; rotate it if it is ever shared. Keep it out of this repository.
- **L4. GDPR.** The Privacy notice covers PDPL, DIFC and ADGM. For EU visitors (the site is offered in French), add one line: "If you are in the EU or UK, we rely on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)) and you may complain to your local data protection authority."
- **L5. AI imagery disclosure.** The redesign films and the Recall film use AI-generated scenes and fictional people. That isn't required by UAE law, but it's advisable for trust and consumer protection. *Fix:* footer or caption line: "Some images and films on this site are AI-generated; people shown are fictional."
- **L6. Recall wording.** "Lane and Watch train attention" and "Your results build up over the weeks" are acceptable. Keep "A training app, not a medical product" next to every Recall call to action, and avoid "improve", "sharper", "brain".
- **L7. Personal mobile number published** on every legal page and Contact. This is the owner's choice. Expect spam calls, and consider a business number once licensed.
- **L8. Redesign QR code.** Confirm it encodes exactly `https://altiora-recall.expo.app/` before going live.

## Checked and fine
- Third-party scripts: Webflow CDN, jQuery 3.5.1 (Webflow default, with SRI), Google webfont loader, Calendly (M3). No analytics or ads.
- HTTPS: http→https redirect, HSTS, `nosniff`; no mixed content.
- No secret keys in the live pages, `alto/` files or full git history (only placeholder names for Script properties).
- Alto: typed text is never sent or stored; answers are inserted with `textContent`; links are fixed.
- /tessera returns 401 and the password page contains none of the protected content. Webflow page passwords are a shared secret with no per-person access or audit trail, so treat the page as "unlisted", not secure.
- Redesign: `altiora-path` is checked against the known values before use; reduced-motion is supported; the external link uses `rel="noopener"`.
- Legal pages: pre-licence statements, PDPL basis, retention, rights and the English-prevails note are present.
