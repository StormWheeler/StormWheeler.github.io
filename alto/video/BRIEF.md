# Altiora AI video brief

Three short films, made with Higgsfield. Drafted 19 Nov 2026.

## Ground rules for every film

- **Pre-licence.** No prices, no "book now", no client names, no results we can't show. Altiora AI is planned, not trading. End cards say what's true: "Coming to the UAE", "Free beta", "Private preview".
- **Tessera stays private.** The Tessera film is for meetings with hotels, not for social media or the public site. Prompts and clips sit in the Higgsfield account, so nothing secret goes into them: no technical details, partners or pricing.
- **Recall is a training app, not a medical product.** No claims about brain health, dementia, IQ or treatment.
- **Fictional people only.** No real guests, staff or public figures. No real hotel brands or logos.
- **Real screens are added in the edit.** AI video garbles on-screen text, so phone screens are shot blank or blurred, and the real UI (Recall's screens, the Altiora and Tessera marks) is laid in afterwards.
- **Look and feel.** Calm and premium: slow camera moves, warm evening light, Gulf architecture. No neon or "hacker" visuals.
  - Altiora: deep forest green and gold.
  - Tessera: Altiora forest with the gold and green token.
  - Recall: navy, soft blue and gold.

## How we make them

1. **Style frames.** One still per shot, very cheap. You approve the look.
2. **Drafts.** Each shot as a 480p draft clip. You approve the motion.
3. **Finals.** Only the approved drafts are rendered at 1080p.
4. **Edit.** Shots are cut together with title cards, the real logos and screens, music and subtitles. Then 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 versions are exported.
